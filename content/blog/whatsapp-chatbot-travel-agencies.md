## H1: WhatsApp Chatbot for Travel Agencies

A travel agency in Pakistan spends most of its day quoting people who were never going to book. Someone asks the Umrah package price, receives a detailed quote assembled over ten minutes, and disappears. Multiply by forty a day.

The problem is not the enquiries. It is that quoting happens before qualifying — and qualifying is exactly the part a machine can do at 11pm without getting tired.

---

## The Enquiries That Consume the Day

- Umrah package prices, by month and hotel category
- Visa requirements and processing times for a specific country
- Ticket prices for a route and date
- Documents needed for a visa application
- Whether a particular country is currently issuing
- Hajj registration and quota questions
- Group and family package pricing

Most of these are answerable from information you already have. What makes them expensive is that a person answers each one individually, in real time, while another twenty messages queue.

---

## Qualify First, Then Quote

The single highest-value change is inverting the order. Before any price is discussed, establish:

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Destination or service** | Umrah, visa, ticketing, tour — completely different workflows |
| **Travel dates or month** | Pricing varies enormously by season |
| **Number of travellers, and ages** | Changes package and visa requirements |
| **Passport status** | Valid, expiring, or not yet issued — a genuine disqualifier |
| **Previous travel history** | Materially affects visa likelihood for many countries |
| **City of departure** | Affects ticketing and transfers |
| **Budget range** | Filters economy from premium before you build a quote |

Seven questions, answered in chat, in under three minutes. A consultant then opens a conversation already knowing whether this is a real booking.

## What the Bot Can Answer Directly

**Standard package information** — what is included, hotel categories, durations, typical inclusions and exclusions.

**Document checklists** — what is required for a given visa type. These are stable, frequently requested, and currently sent manually dozens of times a week.

**Processing timelines** — indicative, clearly labelled as indicative.

**Office details** — location, timings, contact.

**Booking process** — the steps, what payment is required when, what happens after.

**Document collection** — accepting passport scans and photographs directly in chat, which is how it already happens informally.

---

## What Must Reach a Person

- **Any final price quote.** Fares and package costs move constantly; a bot quoting a stale price creates a dispute you will lose.
- **Visa approval likelihood.** Never let automation suggest someone will or will not get a visa.
- **Refunds, cancellations and date changes.**
- **Anything about an in-progress application.**
- **Complaints**, especially from travellers currently abroad.

That first one deserves emphasis. Pricing in travel is volatile enough that automated quoting is a liability rather than a convenience.

---

## Pakistan-Specific Considerations

**Umrah is seasonal and enormous.** Enquiry volume around Ramadan and school holidays overwhelms manual handling completely. Automation earns its cost in these windows alone.

**Roman Urdu is the default.** Enquiries arrive as "umrah ka package kitne ka hai", "visa lagta hai ya nahi". The system must handle this natively, not as an edge case.

**Voice notes are extremely common** in this sector, more than most. Decide explicitly whether to transcribe or route them — ignoring them loses genuine bookings.

**Document sharing happens in chat already.** Passport photos, CNIC images and previous visa copies are routinely sent on WhatsApp. Build the flow around it, and decide deliberately where those images are stored and who can access them. These are sensitive identity documents.

**Group and family bookings** dominate. Single-traveller flows will not fit most Pakistani enquiries.

**Trust sensitivity is high.** The sector has a reputation problem around advance payments and undelivered services. Automated responses should be transparent that a human will confirm everything, and should never overstate certainty.

**Airline and embassy changes are frequent.** Any information the bot gives about requirements or timelines must be editable by your staff the same day it changes.

## A Sensible Flow

1. Enquiry arrives — from a Facebook ad, Google Business Profile, or a shared number
2. Greeting: automated assistant, human available, office hours stated
3. Service selection: Umrah, visa, tickets, tours
4. Qualification sequence for that service
5. Standard information delivered — inclusions, document checklist, process
6. **No final pricing** — instead, a consultant is assigned with the qualification summary
7. Document collection where the enquiry is genuine
8. Follow-up for enquiries that went quiet before booking

Step eight matters in travel more than most sectors, because the decision cycle is long and families deliberate.

## What Changes

Agencies that deploy this typically report the same pattern: consultants stop repeating document checklists, the share of enquiries receiving any response rises sharply, and the time spent building quotes for unqualified enquiries falls substantially.

Track one number before and after — quotes issued per booking made. If it drops, the qualification layer is working.

## FAQ

**Can the bot quote package prices?**
It can give standard inclusions and indicative ranges, but final pricing should come from a consultant. Fares change too frequently for automated quoting to be safe.

**Will it work for Umrah enquiries?**
This is the strongest use case, particularly during Ramadan and holiday peaks when volume exceeds manual capacity.

**Can it handle Roman Urdu?**
It must — most enquiries arrive that way. Test it on your own past messages before accepting a build.

**Can customers send passport documents?**
Yes, and they already do. Decide storage, access and retention before enabling it — these are sensitive identity documents.

**Should it advise on visa approval chances?**
Never. Route any approval-likelihood question to a person.

**How do we keep visa requirements current?**
Your staff need direct edit access. Requirements change without notice, and stale information damages trust quickly.

**What about customers already travelling?**
Route immediately to a person. Someone stranded abroad should never meet an automated response.
