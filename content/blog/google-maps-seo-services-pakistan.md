## Phase 1 — Baseline and Diagnosis

Nothing changes until there is a before picture.

- **Grid visibility baseline.** Position measured across a spread of points covering your service area, for your five priority keywords. Because Maps results change with the searcher's location, a single rank check cannot show where you are actually visible.
- **Profile audit.** Categories, services, attributes, hours, photos, description, Q&A, completeness.
- **Pin verification.** Confirmed against your real entrance, checked on a phone, corrected where the pin has defaulted to plaza coordinates.
- **NAP audit.** Every place your name, address and phone appear, checked for format drift.
- **Competitor pack analysis.** Which businesses hold the pack for your queries, their primary categories, review counts and profile completeness.
- **Spam check.** Keyword-stuffed names and fabricated addresses among competitors, documented for redressal.

**Deliverable:** a baseline map, a prioritised fix list, and a realistic assessment of which queries and which areas are winnable.

---

## Phase 2 — Remediation

- Primary and secondary category correction, based on what is actually ranking rather than on how you describe yourself
- Pin repositioning and address formatting, including floor and suite for plaza offices
- Service-area configuration for businesses without a public storefront
- Full profile completion: services with descriptions, products where relevant, attributes, regular and special hours
- NAP standardisation across website, social profiles and directories
- `LocalBusiness` schema and visible, crawlable NAP on the landing page the profile points to
- Verification support, including preparation for video verification

## Phase 3 — Ongoing Work

- **Reviews.** A request process your staff can run, WhatsApp follow-up templates, and replies to every review
- **Posts and Q&A.** Weekly posts, seeded questions answering genuine pre-purchase concerns
- **Photos.** Added monthly; recency matters more than volume
- **Monitoring.** Unauthorised edits, Google's automated updates, duplicate listings, suspension notices
- **Redressal.** Reporting competitor spam where it is clear and documented
- **Re-measurement.** Monthly grid comparison against baseline

## What Is Not Included

Stated plainly, because these are commonly implied elsewhere:

- **No purchased or incentivised reviews.** Policy violations that get filtered and endanger the profile.
- **No keyword stuffing in the business name.** It moves rankings briefly, then gets reverted and reported.
- **No fake or virtual addresses.** High suspension risk under current verification.
- **No ranking guarantees.** Distance from the searcher is a ranking factor nobody controls.

## Multi-Branch Engagements

Chains and franchises need per-location profiles, per-location pages, and careful category and service-area configuration. Without it, branches suppress one another in the same pack — a common and expensive failure that looks like a ranking problem but is an architecture problem.

Additional scope for multi-location: duplicate detection and cleanup, per-branch grid measurement, and consistent NAP governance so individual managers do not introduce drift.

## FAQ

**How is this different from local SEO?**
Local SEO is broader — citations, location pages, reviews, on-site work. Maps SEO focuses specifically on map pack and Maps app visibility. Most businesses need both, and they are usually delivered together.

**Can you guarantee a top-three position?**
No. Distance from the searcher is a ranking factor, so there are areas where a closer competitor will always outrank you. What can be committed to is work, measurement and honest reporting.

**What if my profile is suspended?**
Recovery starts with identifying the cause — usually address issues, category abuse, or a policy violation — then a documented reinstatement request. Success is not guaranteed, and preventing suspension is far cheaper.

**Do I need a physical office?**
No. Service-area businesses rank with the address hidden and service areas defined. You cannot use a fabricated address.

**How many reviews do I need?**
There is no threshold. Compare against the review counts of the businesses currently holding the pack for your queries in your area.

**Will this help if most of my customers come by phone?**
Yes — calls and direction requests are among the primary actions Maps drives, and both are reported.
