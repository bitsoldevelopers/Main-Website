## An Example Validation Sprint

A founder building scheduling software for clinics is unsure whether to position the product around reducing no-shows, saving reception time, or offering patients WhatsApp booking.

**Week 1 — Hypotheses and research.** We synthesise the founder's customer interviews and competitor messaging into three positioning angles, each with a clear target buyer and a measurable success threshold agreed in advance.

**Week 2 — Build.** Three focused landing pages, one per angle, with identical structure and a single call to action: book a demo. Tracking is configured so every visit, scroll and booking is attributed correctly.

**Week 3 — Test.** Small, equal paid budgets targeting clinic owners and managers send traffic to each page. The founder handles demo calls personally and records objections.

**Week 4 — Decide.** We compare demo booking rates, cost per booking and the quality of conversations by angle. The result is a positioning decision based on behaviour rather than opinion — and a list of objections that shape the product page and sales script.

**What the sprint does not do:** produce a brand identity, a content calendar or a large campaign. Those come later, once the answer is known.

For many founders, a sprint like this saves months of building marketing around the wrong message.
