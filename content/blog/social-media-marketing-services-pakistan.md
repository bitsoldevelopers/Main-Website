## The Core Service Components

### 1. Strategy

**Includes:**
- Audience definition — who you are trying to reach and where they spend time
- Platform selection — which platforms matter for your business
- Positioning and messaging themes
- Content pillars
- Goals and measurement plan
- Competitor review

**Deliverable:** a written strategy document, reviewed periodically.

**Warning sign:** a package with no strategy phase, jumping straight to posting.

### 2. Content planning

**Includes:**
- Monthly content calendar
- Post concepts aligned to content pillars
- Seasonal and campaign planning — Ramadan, Eid, sales, launches
- Approval workflow

**Deliverable:** a calendar shared in advance for approval.

### 3. Content production

**Includes, depending on package:**
- Graphic design for static posts and carousels
- Short-form video — reels, TikToks, shorts
- Copywriting and captions, in English, Urdu or both
- Photography or videography shoots
- Story content
- Adaptation of content for each platform

**Deliverable:** a defined number and mix of content pieces per month.

**Key question:** how many videos versus static graphics? Video typically drives stronger reach, and production effort differs significantly.

### 4. Publishing and scheduling

**Includes:**
- Scheduling at appropriate times
- Platform-specific formatting
- Hashtags and tagging where relevant

### 5. Community management

**Includes:**
- Replying to comments
- Responding to messages or routing them to your team
- Handling negative comments according to agreed guidelines
- Monitoring mentions

**Deliverable:** defined response times and coverage hours.

**Key question:** does the package include message handling, or only comment replies? Who handles enquiries outside office hours?

### 6. Paid social advertising

**Includes:**
- Campaign strategy and structure
- Audience targeting
- Ad creative and copy
- Budget management
- Retargeting
- Conversion tracking set-up
- Optimisation and testing

**Note:** ad spend is paid to the platforms and is usually separate from management fees. See [Meta ads management in Pakistan](/blog/meta-ads-management-pakistan).

### 7. Influencer and creator marketing

**Includes, where offered:**
- Creator identification and vetting
- Outreach and negotiation
- Briefing and content approval
- Tracking performance

### 8. Reporting

**Includes:**
- Monthly performance reports
- Insights and recommendations
- Review meetings

**Deliverable:** reports tied to business outcomes — enquiries, leads, sales, traffic — not only reach and followers.

## Typical Package Structures

| Package level | Usually includes | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Content calendar, a limited number of graphics, captions, scheduling, basic reporting | Small businesses needing a consistent presence |
| Growth | Strategy, mixed graphics and video, community management, basic paid social management, monthly reporting | SMEs using social media for enquiries |
| Performance | Full strategy, higher video volume, community management, full paid social management, creative testing, conversion tracking, detailed reporting | Businesses relying on social for sales and leads |
| Enterprise / brand | Multi-platform strategy, shoots, influencer programmes, dedicated team, advanced analytics | Established brands with larger budgets |

Package names and contents vary by agency. Compare deliverables, not labels.

## What Social Media Services Should Achieve

**Business outcomes:**
- Enquiries and leads from social channels
- Sales and bookings attributed to social
- Traffic to website or store
- Lower cost per lead or acquisition from paid social

**Supporting indicators:**
- Engagement from the target audience
- Reach within your market
- Video watch time
- Follower growth in relevant audiences
- Response time to messages

Proposals focusing only on follower growth and likes are measuring activity, not results.

---

## Questions to Ask About Any Proposal

- How many pieces of content per month, and what mix of video and static?
- Who creates video — and are shoots included?
- Which platforms are covered?
- Are captions in Urdu, English or both?
- Does community management include messages? What hours?
- Is paid social included, and is ad spend separate?
- Who owns the pages, ad accounts and content files?
- What does monthly reporting include?
- What is the minimum term and notice period?

---

## Common Gaps in Low-Cost Packages

- **No strategy** — content without direction
- **Template-based graphics** used across many clients
- **No video**
- **No message handling**
- **No paid social**, leaving organic reach to do everything
- **Reports showing only likes and reach**
- **Agency ownership of accounts or assets**

## FAQ

**What do social media marketing services include?**
Typically strategy, content planning and production, publishing, community management, paid advertising, influencer work and reporting — depending on the package.

**Is ad spend included in social media packages?**
Usually not. Ad spend is paid to platforms separately from management fees.

**How many posts per month should a package include?**
It depends on goals and platforms. Quality and video mix matter more than raw post count.

**Do social media services include replying to messages?**
Some do, some only cover comments. Confirm coverage and hours.

**Which platforms should a Pakistani business use?**
Usually Facebook and Instagram, with TikTok, YouTube or LinkedIn depending on audience.

**How should social media services be measured?**
By enquiries, leads, sales and traffic, supported by engagement and reach within the target audience.
