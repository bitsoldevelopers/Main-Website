## H1: BITSOL Marketing for Pakistani SMEs

Small and medium enterprises make up the bulk of Pakistan's businesses — distributors, manufacturers, clinics, schools, retailers, service firms, restaurants, traders. Most of them are run by owners who wear every hat and have no marketing department.

Agency services are often designed for either end of the market: cheap posting packages that do little, or corporate retainers priced for large brands. SMEs sit in the middle, needing real results on budgets that must justify themselves quickly.

This page explains how BITSOL Marketing works with that middle.

## What SMEs Usually Tell Us

The problems are remarkably consistent across industries:

- "We get enquiries on WhatsApp but can't reply to all of them quickly."
- "We tried Facebook ads and got leads that never answered."
- "Our website is old and nobody finds it on Google."
- "We don't know which marketing actually brings customers."
- "We have hundreds of past customers and never contact them."
- "An agency posted for us for six months and nothing changed."

None of these are solved by producing more social media posts. Most are solved by fixing response, tracking and follow-up, then investing in the channels that fit the business.

---

## How We Scope Work for SMEs

### Start with one problem

Rather than selling a bundle of services, we identify the single constraint costing the most revenue and address it first. For many SMEs that is enquiry response; for others it is Google Maps visibility, or ad campaigns that are not tracked.

### Phase the investment

Work is added in stages as each stage shows results. An SME should not need to commit to every channel at once to find out whether an agency is useful.

### Match the channel to the business

A Faisalabad distributor, a Lahore clinic and a Karachi e-commerce store need very different things. We recommend channels based on how each business's customers actually buy.

### Keep reporting simple

Owners do not have time for forty-page reports. Monthly reporting shows enquiries, sales where trackable, what was done, what it cost and what comes next.

## What We Typically Deliver for SMEs

### WhatsApp automation

The most common starting point. An AI-assisted WhatsApp assistant answers prices, availability, locations and common questions instantly, collects customer details, and hands conversations to staff when needed. Built on the official WhatsApp Business API.

Related: [WhatsApp automation for Pakistani businesses](/blog/whatsapp-automation-pakistani-businesses).

### Google Maps and local SEO

For businesses customers visit or search for locally — clinics, schools, showrooms, service providers — a properly optimised Google Business Profile with genuine reviews is often the highest-return investment available.

### Targeted paid campaigns

Facebook, Instagram and Google Ads campaigns with conversion tracking set up first, targeted to realistic catchment areas and measured by cost per qualified enquiry.

### Websites that convert

Fast, mobile-first websites with clear offers, trust signals and direct routes to WhatsApp or calls — built so SMEs own them fully.

### Follow-up and repeat business

Automated follow-up for quotes and enquiries, and simple campaigns to past customers — often the cheapest revenue an SME can recover.

### SEO for considered purchases

For SMEs whose customers research before buying — B2B suppliers, education, healthcare, professional services — search visibility built over months.

## Questions SMEs Should Ask Before Hiring Any Agency — Including Us

- **What will you do in the first month, specifically?**
- **Who will work on our account day to day?**
- **What will you measure, and how will we see it?**
- **Who owns the ad accounts, website and customer data?**
- **What happens to our assets if we stop working together?**
- **Which of your recommendations would you drop if our budget were halved?**

The last question is the most revealing. An agency that can prioritise ruthlessly under a smaller budget understands the business. One that cannot is selling a package.

We would expect SMEs to ask us all of these, and we answer them in writing before any engagement begins. Our broader guide on [how to choose a digital marketing agency](/blog/how-to-choose-digital-marketing-agency) covers the full evaluation process.
