## H1: Social Media Lead Generation

Social media produces leads easily and converts them badly. That combination is why so many Pakistani businesses conclude social does not work for them.

The volume is real. What breaks is everything after the click — weak qualification, slow response, and a follow-up process that does not exist. Fix that and the same campaigns start producing customers.

---

## The Three Ways Leads Arrive

### Lead forms

A form opens inside the platform, pre-filled with the user's details. Frictionless, which is exactly the problem — someone can submit in two taps without much intent.

**Works when:** you follow up within minutes and qualify hard afterwards.
**Fails when:** you export a spreadsheet on Friday and start calling on Monday. By then they have forgotten submitting it.

### Click-to-WhatsApp

The ad opens a WhatsApp conversation directly. In Pakistan this consistently outperforms lead forms, because it lands the person in the channel where they are comfortable and where the sale actually happens.

**Works when:** someone answers quickly, including evenings.
**Fails when:** the message goes to an unmonitored number.

### Comments

The most underrated lead source in Pakistani social advertising. People comment "price?", "location?", "DM me" rather than clicking anything.

These are leads. Unanswered, they are public evidence that you do not respond — visible to everyone else considering you.

## Why Social Leads Feel Low Quality

They usually are lower intent than search leads, and this is structural rather than fixable: you interrupted someone rather than answering a question they asked.

That does not make them worthless. It changes what you must do with them.

| Search lead | Social lead |
|---|---|
| Actively looking | Interrupted |
| Higher intent | Lower intent |
| Fewer, costlier | More, cheaper |
| Converts sooner | Needs nurture |
| Little qualification needed | Qualification essential |

Businesses that treat social leads like search leads — send to sales, expect a close — conclude the leads are junk. Businesses that qualify and nurture them get a different result from identical campaigns.

## The Qualification Layer

Before a salesperson is involved, establish what makes a lead real for your business. For most Pakistani categories that is three or four questions:

- What specifically do you need?
- Which area or city?
- What budget range?
- When are you looking to proceed?

Asked in chat, immediately, while attention still exists. This filters the two-tap form submissions from genuine buyers without a salesperson spending an hour discovering the difference.

---

## Response Speed Decides Most of It

A social lead's intent decays faster than any other channel, because it was never a deliberate search in the first place. Someone who tapped an ad at 10pm is not thinking about you at 10am.

Practical standard: respond within minutes, automatically if necessary, then qualify. For businesses receiving evening and weekend volume — which in Pakistan is most — automated first response is what makes this consistent rather than aspirational.

## Creative That Produces Enquiries Rather Than Engagement

The ad determines lead quality more than the targeting does.

**What increases quality:**
- Stating the price or price range in the creative — filters out the unqualified before they click
- Naming the specific product or service rather than the category
- Naming the area you serve
- A clear, specific call to action
- Showing the actual thing, not a stock image

**What destroys quality:**
- "Comment for price" as a tactic, which generates volume nobody can answer
- Vague offers that attract everyone
- Contests and giveaways, which produce lists of people who wanted a free item
- Targeting so broad it captures anyone

Stating prices is the single biggest quality lever and the most resisted. It reduces lead volume and increases lead value, which looks worse on a report and better in the bank.

## Pakistan-Specific Realities

**Comments are a primary lead channel.** In many categories more people comment than click. Someone must be answering them within minutes, and the reply should move the conversation to WhatsApp.

**Click-to-WhatsApp generally beats lead forms.** It matches how Pakistani buyers behave and removes a step.

**Evening and weekend volume is heavy.** The highest-intent moments happen when offices are closed.

**Roman Urdu.** Enquiries and comments arrive in it. Qualification and replies must work in the language used.

**Cash on delivery affects what a lead is worth.** An order placed is not revenue until it is accepted at the door. Track acceptance, or your cost per customer is understated.

**Seasonality.** Ramadan, Eid and wedding season shift both volume and auction costs sharply.

## What to Measure

Not leads. Not cost per lead.

1. **Cost per qualified lead** — against your written definition
2. **Response time**, median
3. **Percentage of leads and comments receiving any response**
4. **Cost per closed customer**, by campaign

The third one is usually the number that explains disappointing results. Most businesses have never measured it and are surprised.

Social media lead generation in Pakistan is rarely a targeting problem. It is a qualification and response problem wearing a targeting costume.

## FAQ

**Are social media leads worth it?**
Yes, at lower intent and lower cost than search. They require qualification and nurture that search leads often do not.

**Lead forms or click-to-WhatsApp?**
In Pakistan, click-to-WhatsApp generally converts better because it matches how buyers behave.

**Why are our social leads poor quality?**
Usually vague creative, no price signalling, broad targeting, or no qualification layer before sales.

**How fast should we respond?**
Minutes. Social lead intent decays faster than any other channel.

**Should we put prices in ads?**
Usually yes. It reduces volume and raises quality, which improves cost per customer even as cost per lead rises.

**What about comments asking for price?**
They are leads. Answer within minutes and move the conversation to WhatsApp.

**Do giveaways generate leads?**
They generate lists of people who wanted a free item. Rarely worth the cost.
