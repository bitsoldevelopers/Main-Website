## What a WhatsApp Business API Service Covers

### 1. Meta Business verification

Your business must be verified in Meta Business Manager using registration documents that match your business name and details. Mismatches between documents, website and business profile are the most common cause of delays for Pakistani businesses.

A good provider checks documents before submission and guides corrections.

### 2. Phone number onboarding

- Choosing a number — new, or migrating an existing one from the Business app
- Understanding that a number registered on the API cannot simultaneously be used in the regular WhatsApp or Business app
- Display name approval
- Business profile set-up — description, address, website, category, hours

Migration needs planning: chat history in the app does not transfer to the API.

### 3. Message template creation and approval

Business-initiated messages — notifications, reminders, campaigns — require templates approved by Meta.

- Writing templates in the correct category: marketing, utility or authentication
- Urdu and English versions
- Variables for personalisation
- Resubmitting rejected templates with compliant wording

### 4. Shared team inbox

- Multiple agents on one number
- Conversation assignment and routing
- Labels, notes and conversation history
- Performance visibility by agent

### 5. Chatbot and automation

- Greeting and menu flows
- AI-powered FAQ responses
- Lead capture and qualification
- Booking, ordering and tracking flows
- Human handover rules

### 6. Integrations

- CRM systems
- E-commerce platforms
- Booking and scheduling tools
- Payment links
- Courier tracking
- Click-to-WhatsApp ads attribution

### 7. Compliance and quality management

- Opt-in collection processes
- Opt-out handling
- Quality rating and messaging limit monitoring
- Commerce policy guidance for restricted categories

### 8. Ongoing support

- Template updates
- Flow and knowledge base changes
- Troubleshooting delivery issues
- Reporting and optimisation

## Understanding API Costs

WhatsApp Business API costs typically have several components:

| Cost component | What it is |
|---|---|
| Meta messaging charges | Set by Meta, varying by message category and recipient country |
| Platform or provider subscription | Access to inbox, automation and tools |
| Set-up fee | Verification support, onboarding, configuration |
| Chatbot and integration development | Building flows, AI and system connections |
| Ongoing management | Support, updates, optimisation |

Meta's pricing model and rates change periodically. Ask providers to separate Meta charges from their own fees so you can compare accurately. Pakistan-specific ranges are covered in [WhatsApp chatbot pricing in Pakistan](/blog/whatsapp-chatbot-pricing-pakistan).

## The Green Tick: Set Realistic Expectations

Many businesses ask for the verified badge immediately. In practice:
- Verification of the business in Meta is different from the official business account badge
- Badge eligibility is decided by Meta based on notability criteria
- No provider can guarantee it

Be wary of anyone promising a guaranteed green tick for a fee.

## How to Choose a Provider

**Ask:**
- Are you an official Business Solution Provider, or do you build on one?
- How are Meta charges billed — passed through transparently, or bundled?
- Who owns the WhatsApp Business Account and phone number?
- Can we move to another provider later?
- Do you support Urdu and Roman Urdu automation?
- Which CRMs and systems have you integrated?
- What support response times do you offer?
- How do you handle opt-in and quality ratings?

**Red flags:**
- Unofficial "API" tools running on personal WhatsApp accounts
- Promises of unlimited bulk messaging to any number
- Guaranteed green tick claims
- The provider owning your business account
- No clarity on Meta charges

## FAQ

**Can Pakistani businesses get WhatsApp Business API access?**
Yes, with Meta Business verification and onboarding through a provider or direct integration.

**Can we keep our existing WhatsApp number?**
Usually yes, but it must be removed from the Business app, and chat history does not transfer.

**Does the WhatsApp API include a green tick?**
No. The official business account badge is decided by Meta and cannot be guaranteed.

**How much does WhatsApp Business API cost in Pakistan?**
Costs combine Meta messaging charges, provider fees, set-up and development. Ask for charges to be itemised.

**Can we send bulk messages with the API?**
You can send template messages to opted-in customers within messaging limits and policies.

**Who should own the WhatsApp Business Account?**
Your business should.
