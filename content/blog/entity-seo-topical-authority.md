## H1: Entity SEO and Topical Authority

Search engines stopped matching strings some time ago. They now try to understand *things* — a business, a person, a place, a product — and how those things relate to each other.

That shift has a practical consequence most Pakistani businesses have not acted on: being clearly identifiable as a specific entity, and being corroborated as one by independent sources, now affects both classic rankings and whether AI systems mention you at all.

---

## What an Entity Is

An entity is a distinct thing with attributes and relationships. Your business is one. So is each of your service categories, each city you operate in, and each person on your team.

Search engines build a model of these and their connections: this business provides these services, in these locations, run by these people, mentioned by these sources.

**Why it matters practically:**

- When someone searches your category, the engine considers which entities genuinely belong in that category
- When an AI assistant answers "who does X in Lahore", it draws on entities it has a confident model of
- Ambiguity — unclear identity, inconsistent details, no corroboration — reduces confidence and suppresses you

Most Pakistani business websites are ambiguous entities. That is the opportunity.

## The Three Layers

### Layer 1 — Declare who you are

Explicit, structured, consistent information about your business.

- `Organization` schema with your legal name, address, phone, and links to your profiles
- `LocalBusiness` schema where you have a location
- The same name, address and phone rendered in crawlable text
- An about page that states plainly what you do, where, and for whom
- Named people with real credentials, not "our team of experts"

**The consistency requirement is absolute.** "DHA Phase 5" and "Defence Phase V" are different strings. A phone number with and without a country code is two numbers. Every inconsistency reduces confidence in the entity.

### Layer 2 — Demonstrate topical depth

Topical authority is not one comprehensive page. It is coverage of a subject area from multiple angles, interlinked so the relationships are visible.

**The cluster shape:**

- A **pillar page** covering the subject broadly
- **Spoke pages** each addressing one specific sub-question in depth
- **Internal links** from every spoke up to the pillar, and across to related spokes
- Coverage complete enough that a reader does not need to leave for a basic follow-up question

A single 4,000-word article on WhatsApp automation demonstrates less authority than fifteen interlinked pages covering pricing, API choice, sector applications, compliance and implementation.

### Layer 3 — Get corroborated

What independent sources say about you carries weight that your own claims do not.

- Directory and association listings with consistent details
- Industry publications and local press
- Partner and client references
- Reviews
- Mentions without links — increasingly counted, particularly by AI systems

## Why Pakistani Businesses Have an Advantage Here

Layer 3 is where most Pakistani businesses are thin, and thinness cuts both ways.

Compared with equivalent Western businesses, a typical Pakistani company has far fewer independent sources describing it — fewer directory entries, minimal press coverage, few association listings, thin review volume.

**That means two things:**

1. Your entity is probably weakly defined, which is suppressing you
2. A modest amount of genuine corroboration goes considerably further here than it would in a saturated market

Very few Pakistani businesses are doing this deliberately. It is achievable now at a cost that will rise as more do.

## Building a Cluster: A Worked Approach

1. **Choose a subject you genuinely have expertise in.** Not a keyword — a subject where you know things others do not.
2. **List every question a buyer asks** about it, from first curiosity through to post-purchase. Use your actual customer messages.
3. **Group them** into a pillar and eight to fifteen spokes.
4. **Write the spokes first.** They are more specific and rank sooner.
5. **Write the pillar** as the comprehensive overview, linking down to each spoke.
6. **Link across** between related spokes.
7. **Keep going.** Authority accrues from coverage over time, not from a single publishing push.

**A useful test:** could a reader answer every reasonable question about this subject without leaving your site? If not, the cluster has gaps, and those gaps are where competitors rank.

---

## Common Mistakes

- **Treating it as a keyword exercise.** Fifteen pages targeting keyword variations is cannibalisation, not a cluster. Each spoke must answer a genuinely different question.
- **Publishing the pillar alone** and expecting authority from one long page.
- **No internal linking.** Unlinked pages are isolated islands, and the relationships never become visible.
- **Inconsistent business details** across pages and profiles, quietly undermining Layer 1.
- **Schema that contradicts the visible page.**
- **Covering a subject you do not actually know.** Depth is checkable, and thin coverage of a broad topic performs worse than genuine depth on a narrow one.

## Pakistan-Specific Notes

**NAP drift is endemic.** Pakistani addresses vary wildly in the wild — block, phase, sector, market naming conventions differ by city and by writer. Choose one canonical format and enforce it everywhere.

**Corroboration sources exist but are underused:** chambers of commerce, trade associations, industry bodies, university partnerships, local business press. Legitimate, accessible, and almost nobody bothers.

**Named people matter.** Pakistani business sites frequently anonymise their teams. Named individuals with real credentials strengthen the entity substantially, particularly in professional services and healthcare.

**Language coverage.** If your audience searches in Roman Urdu, coverage that exists only in formal English leaves part of your topic space unaddressed.

**City entities.** If you serve several cities, each needs genuine treatment — a real page with real local content, not a name swap. A weak city page harms the entity rather than extending it.

## Measuring It

Entity strength has no single metric, but these move as it improves:

- **Number of distinct queries** producing impressions — the clearest signal that your topic space is being understood
- **Brand search volume**
- **Rankings across a cluster** rather than a single page
- **Mentions and citations** across the web
- **Presence in AI assistant answers** for your category
- **Knowledge panel appearance**, where applicable

The first is the most useful and the least watched. A site being shown for a widening set of queries is a site being understood better.

Entity SEO is making it unambiguous what your business is, proving depth across a subject rather than a page, and getting independent sources to confirm it.

## FAQ

**What is entity SEO?**
Making your business clearly identifiable as a specific thing with consistent attributes, so search and AI systems can model it confidently.

**How is topical authority different from ranking one page?**
Authority comes from covering a subject across interlinked pages, not from a single comprehensive article.

**How many pages does a cluster need?**
A pillar plus eight to fifteen spokes is a reasonable starting shape, each answering a genuinely different question.

**Does schema markup create an entity?**
It declares one. Corroboration from independent sources is what makes it credible.

**Why do Pakistani businesses have an advantage here?**
Because almost nobody is doing it, and existing corroboration is thin — so modest effort produces disproportionate results.

**How do I measure entity strength?**
Watch the number of distinct queries producing impressions, brand search volume, and whether AI assistants mention you for your category.

**Can I build authority in a subject I do not know well?**
Not credibly. Depth is checkable, and thin coverage performs worse than genuine depth on a narrower subject.
