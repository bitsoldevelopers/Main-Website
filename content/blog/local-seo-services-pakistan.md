## Who Local SEO Is For

| Business type | Configuration | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Storefront** — clinic, salon, restaurant, retail, academy | Visible address, verified location | Highest. Local pack often outperforms organic entirely |
| **Service-area** — plumbers, electricians, tutors, mobile technicians, event services | Address hidden, service areas defined | High, but needs more review and content support to compensate |
| **Multi-branch** — chains, franchises, multi-city operators | One profile per location | Critical, and easy to get wrong — branches routinely cannibalise each other |
| **Pure online / national B2B** | No physical service area | Low. Standard SEO serves you better |

If you do not serve customers at or from a physical place, local SEO is not your service. Any agency selling it to a pure e-commerce business is padding scope.

## What the Service Should Include

### Profile and pin work

Category selection based on what is actually ranking for your queries, complete services and attributes, correct hours including Ramadan and Eid special hours, photos, and — critically — a pin dragged to your real entrance and verified on a phone.

Pin accuracy is the most under-delivered item in the Pakistani market and has direct revenue consequences: a wrong pin misroutes every customer, and every Careem, Bykea or inDrive driver, to the wrong place.

### NAP and citation consistency

One canonical address format used identically across your website, social profiles, invoices and directories. Pakistani addresses drift badly in the wild — "DHA Phase 5" versus "Defence Phase V", "F-7 Markaz" versus "F7 Markaz" — and inconsistency weakens the corroboration Google looks for.

### Review generation and response

A repeatable process your staff can run: ask at the moment of satisfaction, follow up on WhatsApp with the review link, encourage customers to mention the specific service. Plus replies to every review, positive and negative.

What should never be included: purchased reviews, incentivised reviews, or staff posting from personal accounts. These are policy violations that get filtered and put the profile at risk.

### Location pages on your website

Real pages with crawlable name, address and phone, an embedded map, `LocalBusiness` schema, and content genuinely specific to that area. Not the same page with the city name swapped — that is a doorway page and it does not hold up.

### Measurement across a map

Because results change with the searcher's position, a single rank check is close to meaningless. Proper measurement uses a grid of points across your service area, showing where visibility holds and where it collapses.

## Realistic Timeline

1. **Weeks 1–3.** Audit, category correction, pin fix, NAP standardisation, baseline grid. Little visible movement.
2. **Weeks 4–8.** Profile completion, location page published, review process launched. Impressions and discovery searches typically move first.
3. **Months 3–4.** Position improvements begin, usually widening outward from your immediate area.
4. **Months 5–6.** Competitive queries in dense urban areas start to move. This is the first honest point to judge the engagement.

Anything promising top-three placement in a competitive Lahore or Karachi vertical within a month is describing a keyword nobody searches.

## Pakistan-Specific Complications

**Verification is where engagements stall.** Video verification requires an unbroken recording showing your location, exterior signage, surroundings and equipment. Home-based and shared-office businesses frequently fail. Permanent signage before recording is the single best preparation.

**Plaza and floor addresses.** Offices inside multi-business plazas should put floor and suite in the address line rather than inventing a street address, and must check that the pin has not defaulted to the plaza's registered coordinates.

**Service-area businesses must hide the address.** Listing a home address as a storefront invites suspension. Hiding it and defining realistic service areas is the compliant configuration.

**Competitor spam is widespread.** Keyword-stuffed business names and fake addresses are common in several Pakistani city verticals. The Business Redressal Complaint Form is a legitimate, if slow, response — and a service that never mentions it has probably never used it.

**Seasonal hours matter more than they seem.** A customer who arrives at a shop that Maps said was open often leaves a one-star review, and those are disproportionately damaging to a young profile.

## FAQ

**How is local SEO different from regular SEO?**
Regular SEO competes for website rankings. Local SEO competes for the map pack, where distance from the searcher is a ranking factor you cannot change — which makes measurement and profile accuracy far more important.

**Can I do local SEO myself?**
The profile work, hours, photos and review requests are genuinely doable in-house. Grid measurement, citation cleanup, location page development and schema usually are not.

**How long until I see results?**
Profile corrections can move things within two to four weeks. Competitive urban queries typically take three to six months.

**Do I need it if I already rank on Google?**
Ranking in organic results and appearing in the map pack are separate outcomes. Many businesses rank organically while remaining invisible in the pack that drives calls and directions.

**What if I run several branches?**
Each location needs its own profile, its own page, and careful category and service-area configuration. Otherwise branches suppress each other.

**Are Google reviews really that important?**
They feed both prominence and relevance — review text is used to justify why a listing appeared. Genuine reviews mentioning specific services are worth more than generic praise.
