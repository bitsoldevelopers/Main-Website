## H1: Facebook Marketing Agency in Pakistan

Facebook is still where a large part of Pakistan spends its time online — across age groups, across cities, and well beyond the urban audiences Instagram reaches best.

Yet most businesses treat it as a posting schedule. A designer produces a few graphics a week, someone uploads them, and the page slowly accumulates likes that never become customers.

A Facebook marketing agency should be doing something different: turning the platform into a channel that produces conversations, enquiries and sales. This page explains what that work involves, and how it differs from simply running ads.

---

## Facebook Marketing vs Facebook Ads

The two are related, but not the same service.

**Facebook marketing** is the broader discipline: the page, its content, the community around it, how messages are answered, how reviews and comments are handled, and how organic activity supports paid activity.

**Facebook ads** is the paid distribution layer — campaign structure, targeting, creative testing, budgets and conversion tracking. We cover it separately in our guide to [Facebook ads agencies in Pakistan](/blog/facebook-ads-agency-pakistan).

Most businesses need both, in proportions that depend on what they sell. A restaurant or a salon can generate meaningful demand from a well-run page and community. A business selling to a narrow audience will rely more heavily on ads.

## What the Work Should Include

### Page foundation

- Correct category, contact details, hours, location and service area
- WhatsApp and Messenger buttons configured properly
- A clear description of what you sell and who it is for
- Pinned content that answers the questions customers ask most
- Reviews and recommendations enabled and monitored

A surprising number of Pakistani business pages have outdated phone numbers, broken links or no clear call to action. That is the first thing worth fixing.

### Content that serves a purpose

Every post should do one of a small number of jobs: show the product or service, answer a common question, prove credibility, or prompt an enquiry.

**Content that tends to work on Facebook in Pakistan:**
- Short videos showing the product, the process or the team
- Before-and-after and results content, where genuine
- Customer questions answered publicly
- Offers with a clear deadline and a clear way to respond
- Behind-the-scenes content that makes a business feel real
- Urdu or bilingual captions where your audience prefers them

**Content that tends to waste effort:** generic "Happy Monday" graphics, stock imagery, and festival greetings with no connection to what you sell.

### Community and response management

This is where most of the commercial value sits, and where most agencies do least.

- Comments answered — including price questions, which should never be ignored
- Messages answered quickly, with a defined response time
- Negative comments handled calmly and publicly where appropriate
- Enquiries moved into WhatsApp or a call when that is how sales close

A page with slow replies trains customers to go elsewhere. Automating the first response through a [WhatsApp chatbot](/blog/whatsapp-automation-pakistani-businesses) often does more for sales than an extra post each week.

### Groups and local communities

Local Facebook groups — neighbourhood, city, parent, hobby and trade groups — carry real influence in Pakistan. Participating within each group's rules, rather than spamming them, can build visibility that ads cannot buy.

### Paid support for organic activity

Boosting a post is not a strategy, but targeted distribution of content that already performs organically is. The two should share insights: what works organically informs ad creative, and ad audiences inform content.

## How to Judge Whether It Is Working

**Useful measures:**
- Messages and enquiries received from Facebook
- Response time to those messages
- Enquiries that became sales or bookings
- Engagement from people in your actual market
- Review volume and rating over time
- Cost per enquiry where ads are involved

**Measures that mislead on their own:**
- Page likes and follower counts
- Reach and impressions
- Engagement from outside your service area

A page that grows followers while enquiries stay flat is not working, whatever the monthly report shows.

## Common Mistakes

- **Posting for the algorithm, not for customers.** Volume without purpose does not sell.
- **Ignoring price questions in comments.** Customers read the silence as evasion.
- **No connection between the page and WhatsApp.** Enquiries get lost between platforms.
- **Only boosting posts** instead of running structured campaigns with tracking.
- **Using the same content as Instagram without adaptation.** The audiences and formats overlap but are not identical.
- **Measuring success by likes.**

## FAQ

**Is Facebook still relevant in Pakistan?**
Yes. It retains a broad audience across ages and cities, including many people who use Instagram less.

**What is the difference between Facebook marketing and Facebook ads?**
Facebook marketing covers the page, content, community and message handling. Facebook ads is the paid distribution layer. Most businesses need both.

**How often should a business post on Facebook?**
Consistently enough to stay visible, but quality matters more than frequency. A few purposeful posts outperform daily filler.

**Should our Facebook content be in Urdu or English?**
Whichever your customers respond to. Many Pakistani businesses do best with bilingual captions.

**Do page likes matter?**
Not much on their own. Enquiries, response time and sales are better measures.

**Can Facebook work for B2B businesses?**
It can support B2B through ads and credibility content, but LinkedIn and search usually do more of the heavy lifting.

**How do we handle negative comments?**
Respond calmly, publicly where appropriate, and move the detailed conversation to private messages. Deleting fair criticism usually does more damage.
