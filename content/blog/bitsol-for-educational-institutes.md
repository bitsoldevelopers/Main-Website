## H1: BITSOL Marketing for Educational Institutes

For most educational institutions, a year's financial health is decided in a few weeks of admissions. During those weeks, enquiries arrive faster than teams can handle, parents compare several institutions at once, and a slow reply can mean a lost student — and sometimes a lost sibling in later years.

BITSOL Marketing works with schools, colleges, universities, academies and training institutes to build admissions systems: campaigns that start before the rush, automation that answers every family instantly, counsellor workflows that follow up reliably, and reporting that shows cost per enrolled student.

## What an Admissions Week Looks Like With the System in Place

Consider a school system with three campuses in the middle of its admissions window.

**Monday evening, 9:40pm.** A parent messages asking about Class 6 fees and transport to one campus. The WhatsApp assistant replies within seconds with the approved fee structure, transport routes and required documents, then asks for the child's name, current class and preferred campus.

**Tuesday morning.** The admissions counsellor at that campus opens a prioritised list. The parent's enquiry is marked ready to proceed, with the conversation history attached. The counsellor calls, answers a question about sibling discounts and books a campus visit for Thursday.

**Wednesday.** An automated reminder confirms the visit time, location pin and what to bring.

**Thursday.** The family visits. The counsellor records the outcome in the CRM: application submitted.

**Meanwhile,** a different parent who enquired but did not book receives a follow-up on Friday inviting them to Saturday's open day, and another who chose a different school is recorded with the reason: distance.

**At the end of the week,** the admissions head sees enquiries by channel, response times, visits booked, applications and the most common reasons families did not proceed.

Nothing in that sequence is complicated. What changes is that no family waits overnight for an answer, no counsellor works from memory, and leadership sees what is happening while there is still time to act.
