## H1: AI Marketing Services in Pakistan: The Services, the Deliverables and the Running Costs

There are two costs in every AI marketing engagement and most proposals only show one. Building a system is a project fee. Running it is a monthly bill that scales with usage — messages handled, tokens consumed, integrations maintained.

Businesses that discover the second cost after deployment tend to switch the system off. So this page lists what each service is, what you receive, and where the ongoing cost sits.

## The Services

### Conversational AI agents

An agent that handles enquiries on WhatsApp or your website: understands the question in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu, answers from your actual pricing and product information, qualifies the person, books appointments, and escalates when it should.

**You receive:** conversation flows, a knowledge base built from your material, qualification logic, escalation rules, human handoff configuration, and a test environment before go-live.
**Running cost sits in:** message volume and the underlying model usage.

### Workflow automation

Connecting the systems that currently rely on someone remembering. Lead arrives from Meta, gets enriched and scored, routes to the right salesperson, triggers a follow-up sequence, updates the CRM, and recycles if unanswered.

**You receive:** a mapped current-state funnel, the built workflows, routing rules, and a monitoring view.
**Running cost sits in:** automation platform subscriptions and integration maintenance.

### AI content systems

Not content generation — a system. Briefs derived from search data, drafts produced in a defined brand voice, human editing as a required step, publishing on schedule with internal links already mapped.

**You receive:** brand voice specification, brief templates, editorial workflow, and published content.
**Running cost sits in:** model usage and the human editing time, which does not disappear.

### AI search visibility

Optimising to be cited in AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity answers. Related to SEO but measured differently — citation presence rather than position.

**You receive:** citability audit, content restructuring, entity and schema work, and a monitoring baseline across AI surfaces.

### Predictive and analytical work

Applying your own data to forecast lead quality, churn risk, or creative fatigue. Genuinely useful, and genuinely dependent on having enough historical data — an honest provider will tell you when you do not.

## What Should Stay Human

Worth stating explicitly, because a provider that cannot name these has not deployed enough systems to have been burned:

- Complaints and refunds
- Medical, legal and financial specifics
- High-value negotiation
- Anything where being wrong is expensive or public

Good systems route these to a person quickly and visibly, rather than attempting an answer.

## Integration Reality in Pakistan

Many Pakistani SMEs run on a Facebook page, a WhatsApp number and a spreadsheet. That is a workable starting point, but it changes the sequence — there is no point designing deep CRM integrations for a business without a CRM.

A sensible first phase for that situation: capture every enquiry into one place, respond automatically within seconds, and qualify. The CRM conversation comes after there is structured data worth putting into one.

**Language handling is not optional.** Real messages arrive in English, Urdu script, Roman Urdu and mixtures, with local abbreviations. Insist on testing the agent yourself in the language mix your customers actually use — not in the demo script.

**After-hours capture is the fastest return.** For most SMEs, simply catching and qualifying the enquiries that arrive at 11pm and on Sundays justifies the build before anything more sophisticated is attempted.

## Questions to Ask Any Provider

1. What is the monthly running cost at my expected volume, separate from your fee?
2. Can I talk to a live agent you built, and try to confuse it?
3. What happens when it gets something wrong?
4. Where is our conversation data stored, and who can read it?
5. Which parts do humans still do?
6. What should we not automate?

Question six is the strongest filter in the whole list.

## FAQ

**What is the difference between AI marketing and normal marketing with AI tools?**
Using AI tools makes a team faster. AI marketing services build systems that operate on your behalf and carry defined responsibility for how they behave.

**How much do AI marketing services cost?**
There are two figures: the build and the monthly running cost. Ask for both, and ask what running cost becomes at double your current volume.

**Can AI agents handle Urdu and Roman Urdu?**
Modern models handle both reasonably well, but quality depends on how the system is built and tested. Test it yourself before signing.

**Will this replace my marketing team?**
It replaces repetitive tasks, not judgment. Strategy, offers, creative direction and relationships remain human work.

**What if we do not have a CRM?**
Start with capture, response and qualification. Introduce a CRM once there is structured data worth holding in one.

**How is success measured?**
First response time, percentage of enquiries answered, qualification accuracy, and conversion by source. Message volume alone is activity, not outcome.
