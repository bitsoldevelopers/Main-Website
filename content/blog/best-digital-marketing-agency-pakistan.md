## The Five Kinds of Agency You Will Meet in Pakistan

The market is not uniform, and the mismatch between what you need and what an agency is built to do causes more failed engagements than incompetence does.

| Type | What they are good at | Where they fail you |
|---|---|---|
| **Freelancer or two-person shop** | Low cost, direct access, fast turnaround on single channels | No capacity for multi-channel work; delivery stops when one person is unavailable |
| **Social-media-first agency** | Content production, page management, community | Usually thin on SEO, analytics and paid-media economics |
| **Ads-first / performance shop** | Meta and Google campaign management, tracking | Often no organic strategy, so you rent traffic forever |
| **Full-service agency** | Multi-channel coordination, one accountable team | Quality varies wildly; some are a freelancer network with a logo |
| **Technical / AI-led agency** | Automation, integrations, custom builds, data | Higher cost; unnecessary if your needs are genuinely simple |

Before you shortlist anyone, decide which of these you actually need. A restaurant in Sahiwal needing consistent local visibility does not need the same partner as a SaaS company selling to Gulf buyers.

## What "Best" Should Mean in Practice

Replace the marketing adjective with four testable properties.

### 1. They can describe your funnel before they pitch

An agency that opens with packages before asking how you currently get customers is selling inventory, not strategy. The first serious conversation should be them asking questions: where do leads come from now, what is a customer worth, what is your close rate, what happens to an enquiry after it arrives.

### 2. They measure to revenue, not to vanity

Impressions, reach and follower growth are inputs. Qualified leads, cost per acquisition, and revenue are outcomes. Ask which they will report on. If the sample report is full of reach and engagement, you will spend twelve months feeling busy.

### 3. Their work survives their departure

Good agencies build assets you keep: your website, your Google Business Profile, your ad accounts, your CRM data, your content. Weak ones build on accounts they own, so leaving them means starting over.

**Non-negotiable:** every ad account, analytics property, domain, and business profile must be registered under your business, with the agency added as a user. This single clause has saved more Pakistani businesses from hostage situations than any other.

### 4. They say no to things

An agency that agrees your business needs TikTok, LinkedIn, SEO, Google Ads, a rebrand and an app in month one is optimising its invoice. Focus is a competence signal.

## How Pricing Actually Works

Three structures dominate the Pakistani market.

**Monthly retainer.** A fixed fee for a defined scope. Predictable and the norm for SEO, social and ongoing management. The risk is scope drift — the fee stays fixed while the deliverables quietly shrink.

**Project fee.** One-off work: a website, a brand identity, an automation build. Clear deliverable, clear end. The risk is the handover — confirm who maintains it afterwards.

**Percentage of ad spend.** Common for paid media. Aligns the agency with spending more, not necessarily with earning more, so pair it with a cost-per-acquisition target.

What actually drives the number: how many channels, how much content per month, whether paid media is included, whether development work is in scope, and how senior the people touching your account are. Ask for the fee broken down by deliverable. Any agency that cannot itemise its own scope has not defined it.

## Twelve Questions That Reveal Everything

Ask these in the first meeting. The quality of the answers matters more than the answers themselves.

1. Who exactly will work on my account, and what else are they working on?
2. What does month one look like, day by day?
3. Which metrics will you report, and which will you be held to?
4. Show me a report from a current client with the name removed.
5. What would make you tell me to *not* hire you?
6. How do you decide when a campaign has failed?
7. What happens to the accounts and content if we part ways?
8. Which of your last three clients left, and why?
9. How do you handle enquiries that arrive on WhatsApp after hours?
10. What is the notice period, and is there a lock-in?
11. What do you need from us to succeed?
12. What is realistic in 90 days, and what is not?

Question 5 is the most diagnostic. Anyone who cannot describe a business they are wrong for is telling you they have no method.

## Red Flags

- **Guaranteed rankings or guaranteed lead numbers.** Nobody controls Google's index or auction dynamics. A guarantee means either a meaningless keyword or a coming excuse.
- **No contract, or a contract with no exit.** Both extremes are bad. Look for a defined notice period.
- **Portfolio screenshots with no context.** A traffic graph without a date range, a baseline, or a business name proves nothing.
- **Ownership of your accounts under their email.**
- **Pressure to sign before you have seen a plan.**
- **No questions about your margins.** An agency that does not know what a customer is worth to you cannot set a sensible cost per acquisition.

## Pakistan-Specific Things to Check

**The WhatsApp gap.** Most Pakistani buyers convert in chat, not on a form. Ask how the agency will handle WhatsApp response, routing and follow-up. An agency that generates leads into an unmonitored inbox is generating waste.

**Payment and invoicing.** Confirm currency, invoicing method, and whether ad spend is billed through the agency's card or yours. Paying spend directly gives you cleaner records and control.

**Local search reality.** For anything with a physical location, Google Maps visibility often outweighs website ranking. Confirm the agency actually works on Google Business Profile rather than treating it as a checkbox.

**City nuance.** Buyer behaviour in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Faisalabad and Sahiwal genuinely differs — in price sensitivity, platform preference and how much research precedes a purchase. An agency proposing the identical plan for a Karachi retailer and a Sahiwal academy has not thought about either.

**Ramadan and seasonality.** Ask how the calendar accounts for Ramadan, Eid, wedding season and the academic admission cycle. These move demand substantially in most Pakistani verticals.

## A 60-Day Trial Beats a 12-Month Contract

Rather than committing for a year on a pitch deck, structure a paid pilot:

1. **Define one outcome.** Not "grow the brand" — something like "35 qualified enquiries at under X cost per enquiry."
2. **Agree the measurement before starting.** Which tool, which definition of qualified, who reads it.
3. **Give real access.** Pilots fail when the client withholds information, then blames the agency.
4. **Set a mid-point review at day 30.** Not to judge results, but to judge working style — responsiveness, clarity, whether they raise problems early.
5. **Decide at day 60 on the pre-agreed metric.**

Agencies confident in their process will accept this. Agencies that need twelve months before anything can be judged are asking you to fund their learning curve.

## FAQ

**How much should a digital marketing agency cost in Pakistan?**
It depends on channels, content volume and whether development is included. Rather than benchmarking a number, get each shortlisted agency to itemise its fee by deliverable and compare like for like.

**Should I hire a local agency or an international one?**
For Pakistani market targeting, local knowledge of buyer behaviour, platform usage and seasonality is a genuine advantage. For purely international targeting, judge on relevant market experience instead of location.

**How long before I see results?**
Paid media can produce leads within days. SEO and local visibility typically take three to six months to show meaningful movement. Any agency promising fast organic results is describing something other than SEO.

**Is a freelancer good enough?**
For a single channel with modest volume, often yes, and at lower cost. Multi-channel work with dependencies between SEO, ads, content and automation usually exceeds what one person can sustain.

**What if the agency underperforms?**
This is why the notice period, the pre-agreed metric and account ownership matter. With those three in place, leaving is an inconvenience rather than a crisis.

**Do I need to be on every platform?**
No. Most Pakistani businesses get better returns concentrating on two channels where their buyers actually are than spreading thinly across six.
