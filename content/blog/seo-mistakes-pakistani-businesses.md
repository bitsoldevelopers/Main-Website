## H1: SEO Mistakes Pakistani Businesses Should Avoid

These are not generic errors from an international checklist. They are the ones we encounter repeatedly on Pakistani business websites, ordered roughly by how much money they cost.

Several are invisible until someone looks, which is why they persist for years.

---

## 1. Contact Details Inside an Image

Extremely common and entirely self-inflicted. The business name, address and phone number rendered as part of a designed graphic.

**Why it costs you:** crawlers cannot read it, so your local signals are weakened. Visitors cannot tap the number. Your NAP cannot be matched against your Google Business Profile.

**The fix:** crawlable HTML text, with the phone number as a tappable link. Five minutes of work.

---

## 2. Doorway City Pages

Twenty pages generated by swapping the city name, with otherwise identical content. "Digital marketing in Lahore", "…in Karachi", "…in Multan" — same paragraphs throughout.

**Why it costs you:** this is a recognised doorway pattern. The pages do not rank, and at scale they can damage the site's overall quality assessment.

**The fix:** one genuine page per location you actually serve, containing real local content — local market conditions, areas, examples, considerations specific to that place. Fewer pages, each worth reading.

---

## 3. Deleting Content That Ranks

Redesigns and "cleanups" that remove pages or shorten them substantially, without checking what those pages were doing.

**Why it costs you:** pages that rank do so for reasons. Removing them discards accumulated authority, and there is usually no way back.

**The fix:** before removing or shortening anything, check Search Console for its impressions and clicks. If it ranks, understand why before touching it.

---

## 4. Buying Link Packages

Cheap and widely marketed in Pakistan. A hundred backlinks, guaranteed domain authority increases, "high DA" placements.

**Why it costs you:** the links come from sites with no audience, and remediation later costs more than building a modest clean profile from scratch.

**The fix:** earn links from places with real readers — associations, chambers, industry publications, local press, genuine partnerships. Slower and it does not create a liability.

---

## 5. Keyword-Stuffing the Google Business Profile Name

"Best Digital Marketing Agency Lahore | SEO Services" as the business name.

**Why it costs you:** it moves rankings briefly, then a competitor reports it through the redressal form and the edit is reverted. Anything built on it disappears with it.

**The fix:** your actual business name. Compete on category, pin accuracy, reviews and completeness instead.

---

## 6. Content Rendered Only in the Browser

Modern framework sites where content is assembled client-side and the HTML document arrives nearly empty.

**Why it costs you:** the site looks perfect to you and near-empty to crawlers. Pages never rank, and nobody diagnoses why because the site appears fine.

**The fix:** server-side rendering or static generation. **Check it yourself:** view the page source and search for a sentence you can see on screen.

---

## 7. Hiding Prices

Pakistani buyers search cost terms heavily — "SEO cost in Pakistan", "school fees in Lahore", "website design price". Most businesses deflect.

**Why it costs you:** the traffic goes to aggregators and competitors who answer. You also consume staff time answering the question one message at a time.

**The fix:** publish structures, ranges or starting points. It captures high-intent traffic and reduces enquiry load.

---

## 8. Ignoring the Google Business Profile

Spending on social media while the profile sits half-complete with a wrong pin and four reviews.

**Why it costs you:** for any business customers visit, the map pack outproduces most other digital channels. Neglecting it is neglecting the cheapest visibility available.

**The fix:** correct category, accurate pin verified on a phone, complete services, real photos, genuine reviews, Ramadan special hours.

---

## 9. Checking Rankings From Your Own Office

You search your service, see yourself at position one, and conclude the SEO is working.

**Why it costs you:** local results change with the searcher's location. Your office is the single most favourable place you could test from. Customers two kilometres away may not see you at all.

**The fix:** measure across a grid of points covering your service area.

---

## 10. Judging SEO at Month Two

Spending three months, seeing no ranking movement, and concluding it does not work.

**Why it costs you:** you stop just before the compounding starts, and the spend becomes a write-off.

**The fix:** watch leading indicators — impressions and the number of distinct queries producing them. Those move well before positions do. Judge the engagement at month six.

---

## 11. Publishing Content Onto a Broken Site

Funding four articles a month on a site with crawl or rendering problems.

**Why it costs you:** the content cannot perform, and you conclude content marketing does not work when the foundation was the constraint.

**The fix:** technical audit first. It is usually the cheapest meaningful improvement available.

---

## 12. Multiple Pages Targeting the Same Query

A page per keyword variation, added over years. Three pages all competing for "digital marketing services".

**Why it costs you:** Google picks one, often not the one you would choose, and all three rank worse than a single strong page.

**The fix:** map every target query to exactly one page. Consolidate and redirect the rest.

---

## 13. Writing for an International Audience

Content that reads as though written for a US market — unfamiliar examples, currency, references, and phrasing that does not match how Pakistani buyers search.

**Why it costs you:** it fails to match real queries. People search by neighbourhood, in Roman Urdu, using local terms — marla, kanal, matric, DHA phase, token tax.

**The fix:** write for how your market actually searches and speaks.

---

## 14. No Conversion Tracking

Running SEO and ads with no measurement of what produces enquiries.

**Why it costs you:** every subsequent decision is a guess. You cannot tell which pages, keywords or channels produce customers.

**The fix:** set up conversion tracking including WhatsApp clicks and calls, since that is where Pakistani conversions complete.

---

## The Pattern Underneath

Most of these share a root: **nobody has checked.** The contact details have been an image for four years. The city pages were generated once. The site has rendered client-side since launch.

These are not sophisticated failures. They are unexamined ones, and an afternoon of checking usually surfaces several.

---

## A Self-Audit You Can Run Today

1. Can you select your phone number with a cursor on your website?
2. View source on your homepage — is your visible text there?
3. Search a distinctive sentence from your site in quotes on Google — does it appear?
4. Open your Google Business Profile — is the pin on your actual entrance?
5. Search your main service from a location two kilometres away — do you appear?
6. Do you have more than one page targeting your main keyword?
7. Is there a price anywhere on your site?
8. Does Search Console show impressions growing?

Any "no" is worth investigating before spending anything further.

## Conclusion

The SEO mistakes costing Pakistani businesses most are not exotic. Contact details in images, doorway city pages, bought links, client-rendered content, hidden prices and neglected Google Business Profiles account for the large majority of wasted spend.

Run the self-audit. It takes an afternoon and it usually finds something worth more than a month of new activity.

## FAQ

**What is the most common SEO mistake in Pakistan?**
Contact details inside an image, followed closely by neglected Google Business Profiles.

**Are city pages bad for SEO?**
Not if each contains genuine local content. Pages generated by swapping the city name are a doorway pattern and do not work.

**Is buying backlinks risky?**
Yes. Cheap packages place links on sites with no audience, and cleanup costs more than building a clean profile.

**Why is my new content not ranking?**
Frequently because the site has technical problems capping everything. Audit before publishing more.

**How do I know if my content is indexable?**
View the page source and look for text you can see on screen. If it is absent, it is rendered client-side.

**Should I publish my prices?**
Generally yes. Pakistani buyers search cost terms heavily and the traffic currently goes elsewhere.

**When should I judge whether SEO is working?**
Month six for competitive terms. Before that, watch impressions and query counts rather than positions.
