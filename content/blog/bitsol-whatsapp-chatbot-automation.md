## Why We Built WhatsApp Automation Around Pakistan

Generic chatbot platforms are designed for markets where customers use email and web chat, write in standard English and follow predictable menu paths. Pakistani WhatsApp conversations look different:

- Customers write in **Urdu, English and Roman Urdu**, often mixing all three
- Questions are informal and short — "price?", "kahan hai?", "COD hai?"
- Enquiries peak in **evenings and weekends**
- Customers message **several businesses at once**
- Many sales require **human conversation** before closing
- **Cash on delivery** makes order confirmation essential for e-commerce

Our WhatsApp automation is designed around those realities.

## What the Chatbot Does

### Instant replies at any hour

Responds to customer messages immediately — including nights, weekends and busy periods — using approved business information.

### Understands how customers actually write

Handles Urdu, English and Roman Urdu, including informal phrasing and common variations.

### Answers business questions

Prices, product or service details, availability, locations, timings, delivery charges, policies and required documents — from a knowledge base the business approves.

### Captures and qualifies leads

Asks qualifying questions, records customer details and routes serious enquiries to the right person.

### Takes orders and bookings

Collects order details, confirms COD orders, books appointments, consultations or site visits, and sends reminders.

### Follows up

Sends follow-up messages to undecided enquiries and reminders for pending actions.

### Hands over to people

Routes complex questions, complaints and high-value conversations to staff with the conversation history attached.

## What We Deliberately Do Not Do

- **Use unofficial WhatsApp tools** that risk business numbers being banned
- **Send messages to people who have not opted in**
- **Let the chatbot invent answers** — responses come from approved information, with handover when unsure
- **Remove human access** — customers can always reach a person
- **Take ownership of your WhatsApp account** — it belongs to your business
- **Promise specific lead or sales increases** — results depend on the business, market and follow-up
