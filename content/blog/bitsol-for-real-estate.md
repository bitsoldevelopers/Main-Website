## How We Work With Real Estate Businesses

### 1. Segmented campaigns

We build separate campaigns and messaging for:
- **End-user families** — possession timelines, amenities, security, schools
- **Investors** — payment plans, development progress, location growth factors
- **Overseas Pakistanis** — remote purchase process, documentation, video proof, time-zone-appropriate follow-up
- **Commercial buyers** — footfall, visibility, rental potential

Each segment gets its own landing page and qualification questions.

### 2. Trust-led landing pages

Pakistani property buyers are cautious for good reason. Landing pages lead with verifiable information:
- Approval status and reference numbers
- Developer track record
- Dated construction progress

## Our Standards in Real Estate Marketing

Real estate marketing in Pakistan carries real consequences for buyers. We hold to clear standards:

- **We do not market schemes without verifiable approval status**
- **We do not hide charges** or use misleading "starting from" pricing
- **We do not use renders as if they were completed construction**
- **We do not run aggressive repeated calling or messaging** that damages brand reputation
- **We use official WhatsApp Business API messaging** with opt-in

These standards protect buyers — and they also attract the cautious, high-value buyers who ultimately complete purchases.

## Developers vs Agents: Different Engagements

Developers and property consultancies both sell real estate, but they need different things from marketing.

**Developers** market a specific project over a long sales cycle. Their engagements centre on:
- Launch planning and pre-launch interest building
- Project-specific landing pages with approvals and progress documentation
- Construction progress content over months or years
- Investor and overseas buyer communication
- High lead volume that must be qualified and routed to a sales team

**Property consultancies and agents** market changing inventory across many projects and areas. Their engagements centre on:
- Personal and agency brand building around area expertise
- Portal listing quality and response speed
- Fast WhatsApp handling of enquiries across many listings
- Buyer requirement capture so new inventory can be matched to existing leads
- Referral and repeat-client marketing

**What both share** is the need for instant response, structured qualification and a CRM that survives staff turnover. Salespeople and agents change; the lead history should not leave with them.

When a client is both — a developer with an in-house sales and resale arm — we structure campaigns and reporting separately so project marketing and resale activity do not distort each other's numbers.
