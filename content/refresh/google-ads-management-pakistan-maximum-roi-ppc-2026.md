## H1: Google Ads Management Services in Pakistan

Google Ads is the most direct channel available to a Pakistani business: someone types what they want, and you appear. It is also the easiest place to lose money quietly, because the platform will happily spend a budget on searches that will never convert.

The difference between the two outcomes is management. This page sets out what proper Google Ads management includes, so you can judge whether an account — your own or an agency's — is being run properly.

For choosing a provider, see [Google Ads agency in Pakistan](/blog/google-ads-agency-pakistan).

---

## 1. Conversion Tracking Before Any Spend

Nothing else matters if the account cannot tell which clicks produced business.

**Management should set up:**
- Conversion actions for the outcomes that matter — purchases, qualified leads, calls, WhatsApp conversations, bookings
- Google tag or Google Tag Manager implementation, verified
- Enhanced conversions for improved accuracy
- Call tracking where phone enquiries matter
- Offline conversion import where sales close later, so bidding learns from real revenue
- Primary and secondary conversion selection, so bidding optimises for the right action

A common failure in Pakistani accounts: every button click counted as a conversion, which trains the algorithm to buy clicks from people who never buy.

---

## 2. Keyword Research for the Local Market

- **Commercial intent terms** — "service + city", "price", "buy", "near me"
- **Brand terms** — protecting your own name from competitors bidding on it
- **Competitor terms**, where worthwhile and compliant
- **Local phrasing**, including transliterated and Roman Urdu variants where customers use them
- **Match type strategy** — broad match with strong conversion data behaves very differently from exact match on a small budget

## 3. Negative Keywords — Continuous, Not One-Off

Pakistani accounts commonly waste budget on:
- "free", "jobs", "salary", "course", "internship"
- Cities and countries you do not serve
- Research-intent terms where you sell, not teach
- Irrelevant product variants

Management should include a **weekly or fortnightly search terms review**, with negatives added at the right level and shared negative lists maintained across campaigns.

---

## 4. Campaign Structure

**A workable structure for most Pakistani businesses:**
- **Brand campaign** — cheap, high-converting, protects your name
- **Core service or product campaigns** — grouped by theme, not one campaign per keyword
- **Location-specific campaigns** where budgets or messaging differ by city
- **Performance Max**, where there is a product feed or sufficient conversion data
- **Remarketing**, for visitors who did not convert

Over-segmented accounts with dozens of thin ad groups starve the system of the data it needs to optimise. Under-segmented accounts cannot control budget by priority. Management means finding the balance for the budget available.

---

## 5. Ad Copy and Assets

- **Responsive search ads** with enough distinct headlines and descriptions to allow testing
- **Messaging matched to intent** — price-led, credibility-led, urgency-led
- **Assets** — sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets, call, location and lead form assets
- **Local proof** — delivery areas, working hours, payment options, WhatsApp availability
- **Urdu or bilingual copy** where the audience responds to it
- **Regular testing** of new variations against current best performers

---

## 6. Landing Pages

Ad management that ignores the destination wastes most of its effect.

- The landing page must match the promise in the ad
- Fast on mobile, over mobile data
- Clear offer, pricing guidance where possible, and trust signals
- One obvious action — call, WhatsApp, form, purchase
- Separate pages per service rather than one home page for every campaign

See [mobile-friendly website development](/blog/mobile-friendly-website-development).

---

## 7. Bidding and Budget Management

- **Starting strategy** — manual or maximise clicks while conversion data accumulates
- **Moving to smart bidding** — maximise conversions, then target CPA or target ROAS once there is enough data
- **Budget allocation** by campaign priority and performance
- **Seasonality planning** — Ramadan, Eid, wedding season, admissions cycles
- **Bid adjustments** by device, location, time of day and audience where the data supports it

## 8. Campaign Types Worth Considering

| Type | Best for | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Search | Existing demand | The core of most Pakistani accounts |
| Performance Max | E-commerce and multi-channel reach | Needs good feeds, assets and conversion data |
| Shopping | Product retailers | Requires a Merchant Center feed |
| Display remarketing | Re-engaging visitors | Low cost, weak as a standalone channel |
| YouTube | Demonstration and awareness | Better for consideration than direct response |
| Demand Gen | Visual discovery | Test after search is working |

---

## 9. Account Health and Compliance

- Policy compliance and disapproval resolution
- Restricted category handling — healthcare, financial services, education claims
- Billing set-up and payment method reliability
- Account access and ownership under the client's Google account
- Regular audits for wasted spend, duplicate keywords and broken links

---

## 10. Reporting

Reports should answer what the business earned, not just what the account did:
- Spend by campaign
- Conversions and cost per conversion
- Qualified lead rate, from sales feedback
- Cost per sale and revenue where trackable
- Search terms insight and negatives added
- Tests run and results
- Next month's changes

## Management Fees: Common Models

| Model | How it works | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed monthly fee | Set fee for a defined scope | Stable budgets, predictable scope |
| Percentage of ad spend | Fee scales with budget | Larger accounts; watch the incentive to raise spend |
| Hybrid | Base fee plus a performance element | Balanced for growing accounts |
| Project or audit | One-off set-up or review | Businesses managing ads in-house |

Ad spend is always paid to Google separately, and the account should remain under your ownership. For broader pricing context, see [digital marketing cost in Pakistan](/blog/digital-marketing-cost-pakistan).

## Signs an Account Is Poorly Managed

- No conversion tracking, or button clicks counted as conversions
- The search terms report has not been reviewed in months
- One campaign, one ad group, dozens of unrelated keywords
- All traffic sent to the home page
- Ads unchanged for a year
- Reporting limited to impressions, clicks and CTR
- The agency owns the account
- No brand campaign, while competitors bid on your name

## Conclusion

Google Ads management is not about secret settings. It is the disciplined repetition of a few things: accurate conversion tracking, relevant keywords, aggressive negatives, sensible structure, tested copy, matched landing pages, appropriate bidding and honest reporting.

An account run that way compounds. One left on autopilot spends steadily and teaches you nothing.

## FAQ

**What does Google Ads management include?**
Tracking set-up, keyword and negative keyword work, campaign structure, ad copy and assets, landing page guidance, bidding, budget management, compliance and reporting.

**How much does Google Ads management cost in Pakistan?**
Fees vary by model — fixed, percentage of spend, hybrid or project-based — and are separate from the ad spend paid to Google.

**Is Performance Max suitable for Pakistani businesses?**
It can work well with a product feed, strong assets and reliable conversion data; it is a poor first campaign for a new account with no data.

**How often should search terms be reviewed?**
Weekly or fortnightly for active accounts, to add negatives and catch waste early.

**Should Google Ads traffic go to WhatsApp?**
For many Pakistani businesses, yes — provided responses are fast and conversations are tracked.

**Who should own the Google Ads account?**
Your business, with access granted to the agency.
