## H1: Facebook Ads vs Google Ads

The honest answer to "which platform should we use?" is that they do different jobs. Google Ads captures demand that already exists. Facebook and Instagram ads create demand among people who were not looking.

Most businesses eventually need both. But when budgets are limited — as they usually are — the order matters, and picking the wrong one first wastes months.

This guide compares the two for Pakistani businesses, then covers the other platforms worth considering and how to split a budget between them.

---

## The Core Difference: Pull vs Push

**Google Ads is pull.** Someone searches "AC repair Lahore" or "school admission Karachi". They have a need now. Your ad answers it. Intent is high; volume is limited by how many people search.

**Meta ads are push.** Someone is scrolling Instagram and sees a product, service or offer. They were not looking for it. Intent starts low; volume is almost unlimited, and the creative has to earn attention.

Everything else — cost, creative, targeting, measurement — follows from that difference.

---

## Side-by-Side Comparison

| Factor | Google Ads | Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) |
|---|---|---|
| User intent | High — actively searching | Low to moderate — discovery |
| Demand | Captures existing demand | Creates new demand |
| Audience size | Limited by search volume | Very large |
| Cost per click | Usually higher | Usually lower |
| Cost per result | Often better for high-intent services | Often better for impulse and visual products |
| Creative demand | Moderate — text and assets | High — video and imagery decide performance |
| Targeting | Keywords, location, audiences | Interests, behaviour, lookalikes, broad with AI |
| Speed to results | Fast where demand exists | Fast, but needs creative testing |
| Sales cycle fit | Strong for immediate needs | Strong for awareness and consideration |
| Conversion path | Website, call, WhatsApp | Website, WhatsApp, Instagram DM, lead form |
| Best measurement | Cost per qualified lead or sale | Cost per delivered order or qualified conversation |

## When Google Ads Usually Wins

**Urgent services.** Plumbers, electricians, AC repair, towing, emergency clinics, locksmiths. Nobody scrolls Instagram hoping to find a plumber while their pipe is leaking.

**High commercial intent searches.** "Price of", "buy", "near me", "book", "service in [city]".

**B2B with specific search behaviour.** Buyers researching suppliers, software or industrial products.

**Local service businesses.** Combined with Google Business Profile and Maps visibility.

**Brand protection.** Bidding on your own name so competitors do not take those clicks.

**Considered purchases with research.** Education, healthcare procedures, legal services, property in specific areas.

---

## When Meta Ads Usually Win

**Products people do not search for.** New categories, novel gadgets, gift items, fashion pieces.

**Visual and lifestyle products.** Clothing, jewellery, home décor, beauty, food.

**Impulse purchases.** Low-consideration items with attractive price points.

**Building awareness at scale.** New brands and new locations.

**Retargeting.** Bringing back visitors, cart abandoners and video viewers cheaply.

**WhatsApp-led selling.** Click-to-WhatsApp campaigns are one of the most effective formats in Pakistan.

**Broad consumer audiences.** Where the buyer profile is wide rather than niche.

## How Costs Behave

Rather than quoting figures that date quickly, understand the pattern:

- **Google click costs rise with commercial intent and competition.** Legal, property, education, insurance and B2B services are expensive per click; informational terms are cheap.
- **Meta costs rise with audience competition and creative fatigue.** Costs typically increase during Ramadan, Eid, 11.11 and wedding season, when many advertisers bid at once.
- **Cheap clicks are not the goal.** A higher click cost that converts at a better rate is the better buy.

**Benchmark against yourself:** record cost per qualified lead and cost per sale for each platform over a full month, then compare. Industry averages from other markets rarely reflect Pakistani costs.

## What Each Platform Demands From You

**Google Ads demands:**
- Accurate conversion tracking
- Continuous negative keyword work
- Landing pages matching search intent
- Patience with a smaller but higher-intent audience

**Meta ads demand:**
- A steady supply of fresh creative, especially video
- Fast response to WhatsApp and DM enquiries
- Tolerance for testing several angles before finding a winner
- Accurate conversion data, including delivered rather than placed orders

A business that cannot produce video will underperform on Meta. A business without conversion tracking will waste money on both.

---

## The Other Platforms Worth Considering

**TikTok.** Strong for younger audiences, food, fashion, value products and anything with visual novelty. Creative style differs from Instagram; repurposed polished ads perform poorly.

**LinkedIn.** The main B2B option where deal values justify higher costs. Targeting by job title and company is unmatched, but volume is small and costs are high.

**YouTube.** Useful for demonstration, walkthroughs and education, particularly real estate and courses.

**WhatsApp.** Not an ad platform itself, but the conversion layer for most Pakistani campaigns, and a direct channel to opted-in customers.

**Google Maps and local search.** For businesses customers visit, profile visibility and reviews often outperform paid advertising entirely — and cost nothing but effort.

## Choosing: A Decision Checklist

1. **Do people search for what you sell?** Yes → start with Google Search.
2. **Is your product visual or impulse-driven?** Yes → start with Meta.
3. **Is your customer a business decision-maker?** Consider Google Search plus LinkedIn.
4. **Do customers visit your premises?** Google Business Profile and Maps first, before any paid budget.
5. **Can you produce video regularly?** If not, Meta will underdeliver.
6. **Is your average sale value high?** Higher click costs on Google and LinkedIn become affordable.
7. **Do enquiries convert on WhatsApp?** Prioritise click-to-WhatsApp campaigns and fast response.

---

## Splitting the Budget

**If you can only fund one platform:** choose the one matching your primary answer above, and run it properly rather than splitting thinly.

**When you can fund both:**
- Start with the platform that captures existing demand, usually Google Search for services
- Add Meta for demand creation and retargeting once the first is profitable
- Keep a portion for retargeting on both, which is usually the cheapest conversion source
- Review the split monthly by cost per sale, not by cost per click

**Seasonal shifts:** move budget towards Meta during high-demand shopping periods, and towards search during admission cycles, tax seasons or other intent-driven peaks in your sector.

---

## Using Both Together

- Meta video builds awareness; Google captures the branded searches that follow
- Google search visitors who did not convert are retargeted on Meta
- Meta audiences are built from website visitors driven by search
- Brand campaigns on Google protect the demand Meta created

Businesses that track branded search volume often find Meta campaigns quietly increasing it — an effect last-click reporting attributes to Google.

---

## Common Mistakes

- **Judging platforms on cost per click** instead of cost per sale
- **Running Meta ads with a single static image** and concluding the platform does not work
- **Sending all Google traffic to the home page**
- **No retargeting on either platform**
- **Ignoring Google Business Profile** while paying for clicks
- **Counting placed COD orders as conversions**
- **Switching platforms every few weeks** before either has enough data

## Conclusion

Google Ads captures demand; Meta ads create it. The right choice depends on whether people search for what you sell, how visual it is, who buys it and what you can produce creatively.

Run one platform well before adding another, judge both on cost per sale rather than cost per click, and remember that in Pakistan the decisive factor is often what happens after the click — how quickly someone replies on WhatsApp.

## FAQ

**Which is better, Facebook ads or Google Ads?**
Neither universally. Google suits existing demand and urgent services; Meta suits discovery, visual products and awareness.

**Which is cheaper?**
Meta usually has lower click costs, but cost per sale is what matters and varies by business.

**Which platform is best for Pakistani businesses?**
It depends on the business. Local services often do best on Google and Maps; consumer products on Meta with WhatsApp conversion.

**Can we run both on a small budget?**
Usually better to run one properly first, then add the second once it is profitable.

**Where does WhatsApp fit?**
As the conversion layer for both platforms, through click-to-WhatsApp ads and fast replies.

**Should we try TikTok or LinkedIn instead?**
TikTok for younger consumer audiences, LinkedIn for B2B with high deal values — after the core platform is working.
