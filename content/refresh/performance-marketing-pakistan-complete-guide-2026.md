## H1: Performance Marketing in Pakistan

Performance marketing is marketing held to a number. Every rupee spent is expected to produce a measurable result — an order, a qualified lead, a booking — at a cost the business can afford.

That accountability is the appeal. It is also why performance marketing exposes weaknesses elsewhere: if margins are thin, if tracking is broken, or if nobody answers enquiries quickly, the numbers show it within weeks.

This guide covers how performance marketing actually works in Pakistan: the channels, the funnel, the economics that decide viability, the tracking it requires, and how to judge whether it is working.

---

## What Performance Marketing Is — and Is Not

**It is:** paid acquisition measured against defined business outcomes, optimised continuously on that data.

**It is not:**
- **Brand advertising** — valuable, but measured differently and over longer periods
- **Any campaign with a report attached** — reporting reach and clicks is not performance marketing
- **A guarantee of profitability** — it makes results visible, including unprofitable ones
- **Set and forget** — it depends on continuous testing and adjustment

## The Economics That Decide Viability

Before choosing channels, establish whether paid acquisition can work for your business at all.

**1. Contribution margin per sale.** Revenue minus product cost, payment fees, delivery and returns. This is what is available to pay for marketing and profit.

**2. Break-even ROAS.** If your contribution margin is 40% of revenue, you break even at roughly 2.5 times return on ad spend. Below that, every sale loses money.

**3. Customer acquisition cost (CAC) ceiling.** The most you can pay for a customer while staying profitable, given your margin.

**4. Repeat purchase and lifetime value.** Businesses with strong repeat rates can afford a higher first-purchase CAC; one-off purchase businesses cannot.

**5. Payback period.** How long it takes to recover acquisition cost from a customer's contribution. Short payback protects cash flow.

Businesses that skip this step often scale campaigns that were never profitable.

## The Core Channels

### Google Search Ads

**Strength:** captures existing demand. Someone searching "solar installation Lahore" already wants the service.

**Best for:** services, considered purchases, urgent needs, B2B.

**Watch:** irrelevant search terms wasting budget; competitors bidding on brand names.

See [Google Ads management in Pakistan](/blog/google-ads-management-pakistan-maximum-roi-ppc-2026).

### Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram)

**Strength:** creates demand and reaches broad audiences by interest and behaviour; strong for visual products and click-to-WhatsApp conversion.

**Best for:** e-commerce, consumer services, real estate, education, restaurants.

**Watch:** creative fatigue; optimising towards low-quality leads or unconfirmed orders.

See [Meta ads management in Pakistan](/blog/meta-ads-management-pakistan).

### Google Shopping

**Strength:** product listings shown to people searching for specific items.

**Best for:** e-commerce with clean product feeds.

### YouTube

**Strength:** demonstration and storytelling at low cost per view; supports consideration.

**Best for:** products needing explanation, real estate walkthroughs, education.

### LinkedIn

**Strength:** targeting by job title, company and industry.

**Best for:** B2B services and software with sufficient deal value to justify higher costs.

### Affiliate and creator partnerships

**Strength:** payment tied to results; creator content doubles as ad creative.

**Watch:** attribution disputes and coupon misuse; agree tracking rules in writing.

### Programmatic display and retargeting

**Strength:** inexpensive reach and retargeting of engaged visitors.

**Watch:** poor placements and inflated view-through attribution.

## Structuring the Funnel

**Top — demand creation.** Broad reach with video and strong creative. Measured by qualified reach, watch time and resulting audience growth, not immediate sales.

**Middle — consideration.** Retargeting engaged viewers and visitors with proof, comparisons, offers and answers to objections.

**Bottom — conversion.** High-intent search, retargeting of cart or pricing-page visitors, click-to-WhatsApp campaigns and direct offers.

Businesses that run only bottom-funnel campaigns exhaust existing demand and watch costs climb. Those that run only top-funnel campaigns generate attention without sales.

## Tracking: The Foundation

Performance marketing is only as accurate as its measurement.

- **Conversion tracking** on every platform, tied to real outcomes
- **Server-side tracking** — Meta Conversions API, Google enhanced conversions — for reliability
- **UTM parameters** on every link
- **CRM integration** so leads can be traced to sales
- **WhatsApp conversation tracking**, since many Pakistani conversions happen there
- **Call tracking** where phone calls matter
- **Offline conversion upload** where sales close outside digital channels

**COD adjustment:** in Pakistani e-commerce, a placed order is not revenue. Campaigns should be evaluated on delivered orders, and where possible that outcome should be fed back to the ad platforms so they optimise for customers who actually accept delivery.

## KPIs Worth Tracking

| KPI | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Cost per qualified lead | Acquisition efficiency for lead businesses |
| Cost per delivered order | True e-commerce acquisition cost |
| ROAS, compared against break-even ROAS | Whether campaigns are profitable |
| CAC versus CAC ceiling | Whether you can afford to scale |
| Contribution margin after ad spend | What the business keeps |
| Lead-to-sale rate by channel | Lead quality |
| Payback period | Cash flow safety |
| Repeat purchase rate by acquisition channel | Long-term value of each channel |

A campaign with a lower ROAS but higher repeat purchase rate can be worth more than one with a flattering first-order number.

## Creative: The Main Lever

With platform algorithms handling most targeting decisions, creative quality now drives most performance differences.

- Test several distinct angles, not colour variations
- Short video showing the product, process or result usually outperforms static images
- Real customers and real premises outperform stock imagery
- Refresh creative before fatigue sets in
- Match the language customers use, including Urdu and Roman Urdu

---

## Speed of Response Is Part of Performance

In Pakistan, a large share of paid campaigns end in a WhatsApp message or a phone call. A campaign producing enquiries that wait hours for a reply is being judged on half its funnel.

Instant response and qualification should be treated as part of the performance marketing system, not as a separate concern. See [WhatsApp chatbot for lead qualification](/blog/whatsapp-chatbot-lead-qualification).

## Agency Models and Fees

| Model | How it works | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed monthly fee | Set fee for defined scope | Scope creep or under-servicing |
| Percentage of ad spend | Fee scales with budget | Incentive to increase spend |
| Performance-based | Fee tied to results | Requires airtight tracking and agreed definitions |
| Hybrid | Base fee plus performance element | Usually the most balanced |

Keep ad accounts under your own ownership in every case.

## Common Mistakes

- **Scaling before unit economics are known**
- **Optimising for cheap clicks or cheap leads**
- **Counting placed COD orders as revenue**
- **Running only conversion campaigns until audiences are exhausted**
- **Ignoring creative fatigue**
- **No retargeting**
- **Slow enquiry response**
- **Judging channels on last-click attribution alone**

## FAQ

**What is performance marketing?**
Paid marketing measured and optimised against defined business outcomes such as qualified leads, orders or bookings.

**How is it different from digital marketing generally?**
Digital marketing includes brand, content, social and SEO. Performance marketing is the accountable, paid acquisition subset.

**What ROAS should we aim for?**
Above your break-even ROAS, which depends on your contribution margin. There is no universal target.

**Which channel is best in Pakistan?**
It depends on the business — Google Search for existing demand, Meta for demand creation and WhatsApp conversion, LinkedIn for B2B.

**How does cash on delivery affect performance marketing?**
Refused deliveries mean placed orders overstate revenue. Measure delivered orders.

**How much budget is needed to start?**
Enough to gather meaningful data on at least one channel over several weeks; the amount depends on your cost per result.
