## 0. Fix these three things before submitting anywhere

A citation is only worth something if it is consistent. Right now three
different versions of the business are circulating, so every new submission
would multiply the confusion.

**1. Pick one address and one legal name.** In public right now:

| Source | Name | Address |
|---|---|---|
| bitsolmarketing.com schema | BITSOL Marketing PVT LTD | 83/3 C KB Commercial, Phase 1, DHA, Lahore 54792 |
| Jamals Yellow Pages, Bizrar | Bitsol Marketing Pvt. Ltd. | 83/3 Commercial, KB Colony, Airport Road, Lahore |
| Crunchbase | Bharwana IT Solutions (SMC PVT) LTD | Samundri |

Decide which is the registered trading address, then use it verbatim everywhere.
Google treats "KB Colony, Airport Road" and "KB Commercial, Phase 1, DHA" as two
different businesses.

**2. Resolve the duplicate Facebook page.** The site links
`facebook.com/bitsolmarketing`, while search surfaces
`facebook.com/bitsolmarketingpk`. Merge or delete one, then list only the
survivor in every profile.

**3. Move `public/disavow.txt` out of the public folder.** Disavow files are
uploaded inside Search Console. Hosting it at `bitsolmarketing.com/disavow.txt`
publishes the list of spam networks previously pointed at the domain, for
anyone who looks. Confirm the file was actually submitted in Search Console
while you are there.

## 2. Submit, in this order

### Tier A — Pakistani authority (nothing a competitor can buy)

| Target | URL | Cost | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pakistan Software Export Board | [portal.techdestination.com](https://portal.techdestination.com/auth/register) | Free | Company registration, NTN, gets you into the official IT company directory |
| P@SHA membership | [pasha.org.pk/apply-membership](https://www.pasha.org.pk/apply-membership/) | Annual fee | Registered place of business in Pakistan, expires every 31 March |
| Lahore Chamber of Commerce | [lcci.com.pk/membership.php](https://lcci.com.pk/membership.php) | Annual fee | NTN, Lahore jurisdiction, two existing members to propose and second |

These are the strongest links available to you, because they require being a
real registered Pakistani company.

### Tier B — Agency directories

| Target | URL | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Clutch (Basic) | [clutch.co/get-listed](https://clutch.co/get-listed) | Free tier, $499/yr verified |
| GoodFirms | [goodfirms.co/get-listed](https://www.goodfirms.co/get-listed) | Free, four-step verification |
| The Manifest | Comes with the Clutch profile | Free |
| UpCity, Sortlist, FindBestFirms | Search "get listed" on each | Free tiers |
| DesignRush | Paid from $1,500/yr | Skip until the free tiers are done |

Note: DesignRush already lists a different company called "Bitsol Technologies".
Use the full name "BITSOL Marketing" everywhere to avoid being conflated with it.

### Tier C — Local and map

Google Business Profile, [Bing Places](https://www.bingplaces.com) and
[Apple Business Connect](https://businessconnect.apple.com). All free, all feed
the map pack, all need the address from step 0 to match exactly.

### Tier D — Product-led, for ConversIQ

The chatbot product can earn links the agency cannot:
[Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/new), [SaaSHub](https://www.saashub.com/submit),
AlternativeTo, G2 and Capterra. A launch gets coverage; an agency listing does not.

### Tier E — Earned coverage

[Help a B2B Writer](https://helpab2bwriter.com), Featured and Qwoted send daily
questions from journalists. Answering three a week with specific Pakistani market
data is the most reliable way to earn editorial links. Pitch TechJuice,
ProPakistani and Startup Pakistan only with original data, not with service pages.

## 5. Tracking

```bash
node scripts/check-backlinks.mjs           # readable table
node scripts/check-backlinks.mjs --json    # machine readable
```

Add every new submission to `content/backlinks/targets.json` as it goes in, then
run the checker weekly. A link that never appears after three weeks means the
submission was rejected, not slow.

---

## 6. What not to do

The `disavow.txt` in this repo lists Fiverr anchor-text networks and PBNs with
Semrush toxicity scores of 88 to 100, so the domain has already been through one
cleanup. Paid link packages, guest post networks that sell placements, and
directory blasts would repeat it. Google's link spam systems neutralise those
links and the cleanup costs more than the links ever returned.
