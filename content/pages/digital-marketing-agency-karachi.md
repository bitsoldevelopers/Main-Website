## Opening Section

Karachi is the largest and most commercially varied market in Pakistan, and the hardest to generalise about. Within one city you have the country's biggest e-commerce operations, the port and its logistics chain, industrial estates in Korangi and SITE, textile and pharmaceutical manufacturing, wholesale markets, a large corporate head-office presence, and consumer districts from Clifton and DHA to Gulshan, North Nazimabad and Malir.

A single marketing template cannot serve that. An online fashion brand shipping nationwide, a chemicals manufacturer selling to industrial buyers, and a clinic serving one neighbourhood need different channels, different measurement and different definitions of success.

---

## How Karachi Differs

**E-commerce concentration.** Many of Pakistan's largest online sellers and D2C brands operate from Karachi. Cash on delivery, courier performance and refused deliveries shape marketing economics more than anything else.

**Trade, port and logistics.** Importers, exporters, freight forwarders, clearing agents and distributors sell B2B through relationships and search — not through consumer social media.

**Industrial manufacturing.** Korangi, SITE and Landhi host manufacturers whose buyers search specifications and capabilities, and whose enquiries increasingly come from overseas.

**Corporate head offices.** Banking, FMCG, pharmaceuticals and telecom head offices create a B2B services market with formal procurement.

**Dense, varied consumer districts.** Spending power and behaviour differ sharply between areas, which makes broad city-wide targeting wasteful.

**Scale of competition.** More businesses compete for the same searches and feeds than in any other Pakistani city, which raises costs and makes measurement discipline essential.

## FAQ Section — Replacement Set

**How do I choose a digital marketing agency in Karachi?**
Ask who works on the account, what the first 30 days include, how results are measured, and who owns the ad accounts. For e-commerce specifically, ask whether they report on delivered orders or only on placed orders — the difference decides whether the numbers mean anything.

**How much does digital marketing cost in Karachi?**
Management fees vary by scope and are separate from ad spend paid to the platforms. E-commerce campaigns, B2B lead generation and local service marketing are priced differently because the work differs.

**Do you work with e-commerce businesses in Karachi?**
Yes, including store development, product SEO, Meta and Google campaigns, WhatsApp order automation and COD confirmation, and retention marketing.

**Can you help industrial and export businesses?**
Yes. That work centres on capability and specification content, search visibility for the terms buyers use, credibility for overseas buyers, and structured enquiry handling across time zones.

**Do you have an office in Karachi?**
*(Answer honestly. If there is no Karachi office: "We are based in Lahore and work with Karachi clients remotely, with a named account contact and scheduled calls." Do not imply a local branch.)*

**How long before we see results?**
Paid campaigns and WhatsApp automation can show movement within weeks. SEO in Karachi's competitive categories usually takes several months.
